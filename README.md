📊 E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard
Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, regional contribution, and shipping efficiency within an e-commerce business.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.
Tools Used
Power BI Desktop
DAX
Power Query
Data Modeling
Excel / CSV Dataset

Key KPIs
Total Revenue: ₹5.28M
Total Profit: ₹1.44M
Total Orders: 10K
Total Customers: 10K

Business Questions Addressed
Sales Analysis
How has revenue grown over time?
What are the high-performing and low-performing periods?


Product Analysis
Which categories contribute the highest revenue?
Which products generate the highest profit?
What is the relationship between discounts and profitability?

Customer Analysis
Which customer segments drive the most revenue?
Which customer groups contribute most to profitability?


Regional Analysis
Which regions contribute most to sales and profit?
Are there regional profitability differences?


Operations Analysis
Which shipping methods are most frequently used?
Does shipping choice impact profitability?

Key Insights
Revenue Growth

Sales increased significantly between 2021 and 2023 before declining in 2024.

Category Contribution

Electronics emerged as the dominant revenue-generating category, contributing over 60% of total revenue.

Discount Impact

Higher discounts did not consistently lead to higher profits, indicating opportunities for pricing optimization.

Customer Segments

Regular customers generated more than half of total revenue, highlighting the importance of customer retention.

Regional Performance

Revenue remained stable across regions, although profitability varied considerably.
